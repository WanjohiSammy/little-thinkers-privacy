Little Thinkers: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Little Thinkers is a learning app for children aged 3 to 8. We built it so that it collects nothing about you or your child.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store or share any personal information, from children or from adults.

What stays on the device

Your child's name and picture (if you add them), stars, stickers, colouring, weekly activity summary and progress, and the app settings (read-aloud and sounds on or off), are saved only on the device, inside the app. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. If you use Android backup, they are included in your own Google account backup (voice recordings are not). Uninstalling the app or using "Reset progress" in the grown-ups area deletes them.

Read-aloud and recorded voices

The app reads questions aloud using the text-to-speech voice that is built into the device.

A grown-up can choose to record themselves reading the stories, from the grown-ups area (behind a grown-ups-only question). Only then does the app ask for microphone access. Recordings are saved only inside the app on that device. They are never uploaded, shared or included in device backups, and they can be deleted at any time from the recording screen or by uninstalling the app. Children cannot start a recording.

Purchases

Unlocking extra volumes is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google handles the payment. We do not see or store payment details. Google's handling of purchase information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Purchases sit behind a grown-ups-only question so children cannot buy by accident.

Internet

All learning content works offline. The app only uses the internet to talk to Google Play when a grown-up buys or restores a purchase.

Children

The app is designed for children and follows Google Play's Families Policy. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing for us to share, sell or delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
